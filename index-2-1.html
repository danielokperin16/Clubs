Friends and progress together

Chat apps are great for friends, but nothing gets made. Courses can offer no sense of direction at times, and they don't encourage active participation the way clubs do. In Clubs you work toward a goal with a small group who care about the same thing, so making friends and making progress happen at once.

Clubs Intern: an alternative to traditional internships

For people who don't live close to their preferred companies, or haven't had the chance to go there in person, a traditional internship is out of reach. Clubs Intern, at $20 a year, is a more convenient, automated version. A club gives you a real project, a mentor who works in the field, deadlines, and feedback, then a verified record of what you did. It isn't a job. It's real project experience you can start today, wherever you live, and Clubs Intern automates your log and turns it into a detailed record of your work and experience.

From sign-up to your first finished project

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Create your account. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian approves it.

  2. 2

    Find a club

    Pick your interests and see clubs that fit, each with a goal, a mentor, and a clear way in.

  3. 3

    Join

    Some clubs are open to all. Others ask for a short application or a level you've earned.

  4. 4

    Do the work

    Hit weekly milestones with your group and log what you made. With Clubs Intern, Clubs AI drafts the log for you.

  5. 5

    Get it verified

    Your mentor checks your work. It goes on your profile, and you decide who sees it.

A record that carries weight

Published standards

Every level is written down in plain terms, so a Level 3 means the same thing in every club.

Named, vetted mentors

Mentors are identified, checked, and accountable for the work they verify. A mentor's employer is shown for context. It isn't an endorsement unless the company has agreed to one.

Credentials you can check

Each verified record names the mentor who checked it, their role, and their organization, with a link anyone can open to confirm it.

Clubs Intern

Clubs Intern is the paid version: a more convenient, automated alternative to a traditional internship, for people who don't live close to their preferred companies or haven't had the chance to go there in person. Clubs AI logs your work for you and turns it into a detailed, shareable record of your experience.

  • Find and join internships
  • Drafts each log entry from what you upload
  • Builds a detailed outline of your work and experience
  • Creates a portfolio and public link that shows who verified each milestone
  • Tags the skills your work shows
  • Summarizes your growth over time

$20 a year

Planned price for Clubs Intern, which may change before launch. Waitlist members get a founding-member discount.

Clubs AI drafts and suggests. You confirm every entry, and your mentor verifies it.

Can't afford Clubs Intern? Partner companies and sponsors help fund seats for students who need them.

Safety is built in

  • Every mentor is vetted before they run a club.
  • Members talk in group spaces. There are no private messages between adults and minors.
  • A parent or guardian approves accounts for members under 18.
  • Work stays private until the member chooses to share it.
  • Anyone can report a concern, and a person reviews every report.

    Privacy notice

    What we collect. Your name, your email address, whether you're a student, parent or guardian, mentor, or organization, and any optional details you type in, like an organization name. We also note which link or club card you came from.

    Why. To send you a confirmation, email you when Clubs launches, and count how many people are interested.

    Who sees it. Only the Clubs team. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

    Where it's kept. In a private spreadsheet that only the Clubs team can open.
